Privacy

A simple, privacy-conscious portfolio.

This website presents professional work while protecting client identity, production information, and visitor data.

Enquiries

The contact form prepares a structured draft in your own email application. The website does not submit or store the form contents on a server. Your email provider handles the message only after you choose to send it.

Information you send

If you email me, I use the information only to understand and respond to your enquiry, discuss a project or opportunity, and retain necessary professional correspondence.

Sensitive information

Do not send passwords, credentials, private keys, confidential business records, personal identity documents, or production database data through the form or by initial email.

Client confidentiality

Portfolio case studies use sanitized demonstrations and qualified metrics. Client identities, identifiable records, credentials, and confidential production details are not intentionally published.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers. It stores one flag in your browser’s session storage so the intro animation plays only once per visit.

External links

Links to LinkedIn, GitHub, and email services are governed by those providers’ own privacy practices.
